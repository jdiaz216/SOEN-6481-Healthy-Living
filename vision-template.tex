\section{Introduction}

This document outlines Healthy Living's goals, stakeholders, and key features. It defines high-level requirements that guide development and keep it aligned with the needs of all stakeholders, following the Rational Unified Process (RUP) framework throughout the project life-cycle. Healthy Living is a web-based application that helps students plan meals, manage groceries, and discover recipes that fit their budget, schedule, and cooking skill, tying meal planning directly to grocery tracking and recipe suggestions. It meets the needs of students, administrators, content moderators, and support specialists, providing a single, easy-to-use platform for everyday meal management.


\subsection{References}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item \href{https://www.bestcolleges.com/research/food-insecurity-statistics-college-students/}{Food Insecurity in College: Facts and Statistics}: Supports the problem statement by showing how widespread food insecurity is among college students and how it affects grades and graduation.
  \item \href{https://educationdata.org/average-monthly-food-spend-college-student}{Average Cost of Food per Month for a College Student}: Supports the budget-related needs, such as the grocery budget feature, with concrete numbers on student food spending.
  \item \href{https://formative.jmir.org/2026/1/e84576}{Understanding Graduate Students' Perspectives on Food Apps to Inform User-Centered Design}: Supports several product features, including grocery lists, recipe suggestions by skill and time, and nutrition tracking, and confirms that shopping-list use is linked to less food waste.
  
\end{itemize}
